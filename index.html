RARE ORE

The Minecraft trivia where the rarest correct answer wins.

How to play

Each round has 7 prompts like “Name a hostile mob” or “Name a block added in 2026”. You have 25 seconds for each.

Any correct answer scores, but rare answers score more. Saying “Zombie” is fine. Saying “Bogged” is better. A wrong guess doesn’t end the prompt, it just costs 3 seconds.
